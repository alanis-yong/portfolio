About Me

After completing secondary school, I began my career in operational and customer-facing roles, where I was later introduced to web development through maintaining a company website using HTML and CSS. Through hands-on experimentation and self-directed learning, I developed a strong interest in software development and problem-solving.

In September 2024, I enrolled in Sigma Coding Bootcamp, a structured, self-paced program focused on full-stack development fundamentals. Since then, I have been building my front-end skills through coursework and practical projects, developing functional web applications using modern web technologies.

Looking ahead, I plan to expand into back-end development with the goal of growing into a full-stack developer. I am committed to continuous learning and to building reliable, real-world digital solutions.
